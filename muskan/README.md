# 💖 For Muskan - A Personalized Digital Gift App 💖

A romantic, emotional, multi-page app experience designed specifically as a digital birthday gift for **Muskan**.

---

## 📱 The 7-Screen Complete Journey (Strictly Non-Scrolling)

1. **Screen 1: The Interactive Heart & Arrow**
   - Soft glowing cream-to-pink gradient with a floating 3D glossy heart in the center.
   - Touch/drag the bowstring down and release (or tap) to shoot the arrow.
   - Hitting the heart bursts into sparkling particles and smoothly transitions to Screen 2 while playing romantic background piano music.

2. **Screen 2: The Tree of Love**
   - Whimsical tree whose canopy is composed of hundreds of animated heart leaves fluttering in a gentle breeze.
   - Staggered romantic phrases:
     - *"made for you, by hand"*
     - *"I love you"*
     - *"and every ordinary day with you"*
   - Click "For You →" to proceed.

3. **Screen 3: The Cinematic Confession**
   - Dramatic contrast: deep, rich vibrant magenta and wine gradient.
   - Large sweeping white cursive typography:
     - *"this one is for you..."*
     - Massive glowing *"I Love You"*
   - Click "Our Memories →" to proceed.

4. **Screen 4: The Photo Collage & Puzzle (8 Real Photos)**
   - 8 polaroid-style photos start facedown with heart locks.
   - Tap each polaroid to flip it over with a 3D animation, revealing your real photo and memory caption!
   - Tap any revealed photo to zoom into full-screen view!
   - (Includes a "Turn All Over" button).
   - Once all are revealed, the cards rotate into an aesthetic collage layout and unlock the final letter.

5. **Screen 5: The Letters (The Final Message)**
   - Clean, elegant reading view.
   - Step through the love letter paragraphs one by one using arrow buttons or pagination dots.
   - Final card reveals the button: **"Unlock Secret Surprise 🔒→"**.

6. **Screen 6: The "Unlock for Surprise" Passcode Page**
   - Pastel-themed lock screen with alternating soft purple and yellow vertical stripes.
   - **Left**: Bold retro purple title *"unlock for surprise"*, circular scalloped wavy-edged frame holding your couple selfie, and cute party mascot.
   - **Right**: 4 square PIN boxes and a digital keypad with a wavy purple border.
   - **PIN**: `1210` (representing 12th date of 10th month).
   - Wrong PIN triggers a subtle shake animation.
   - Entering `1210` triggers a white screen flash and transitions into the Grand Finale!

7. **Screen 7: The Grand Finale (Lightning & Fireworks)**
   - Dark, high-contrast cinematic sky with looping animated multi-colored fireworks and glowing lightning/sparkler flashes.
   - Massive glowing **"I LOVE YOU"** declaration in the center.
   - Subtitle: *"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MY MUSKAN ❤️"*.
   - Large, beautifully formatted love paragraph (customizable in `config.js`).
   - Signature & "Relive The Journey From The Start 💫" button.
